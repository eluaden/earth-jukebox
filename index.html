Earth Jukebox

Listen to NASA Earth data. Every sound comes from a data value.

Hear our changing planet

NASA's Earth Information Center shows our planet through images. Earth Jukebox turns those same images into sound, so the data can be heard, not only seen. It is built to work without sight: with a keyboard, a screen reader, and spoken narration in English, Portuguese and Spanish.

How it works

  1. Pick a track. Each track is a NASA visualization, shown frame by frame.
  2. Learn the sounds. Before the track, you hear the lowest and the highest value of each sound, so you know the scale.
  3. Listen. Every sound is made live from the data value read from the frame: a tone, a wind, a tick, and a bell on records.

Nothing plays until you press a button. Headphones are recommended.
