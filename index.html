Before you press play

Three layers, one planet

Every track has up to three layers. Each one has its own sound, colour and shape, so nothing depends on colour alone.

The steady tone

The main measurement. In A1 it is the ice: lower and quieter means less ice. It is the solid ice-blue tube.

The rushing wind

The change. It grows as the ice disappears. It is the striped coral tube.

Ticks and bells

Time. One tick per year, a stronger one each decade, a bell at every record low. They are the lamps.

Under the hood

From pixels to sound

Nothing is pre-recorded. The jukebox reads the data and plays it live in your browser.

  1. Frames

    We load the frames of an Earth Information Center visualization.

  2. Colour legend

    Each pixel's colour turns back into the value it stands for, using the visualization's own scale.

  3. Values

    The values become one series per variable, frame by frame.

  4. Sound and voice

    The series drive the sound, and a voice gives the context: what, where, when and how much.
